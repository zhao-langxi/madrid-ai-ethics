# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve public-safe notes on systems, language access, and AI ethics in public services.

## Who can contribute

- The maintainer ([Jade Zhao](https://github.com/jadexzhao))
- IU Informatics / study-abroad peers with public-safe observations
- Public-interest tech builders improving clarity, links, or reading lists

## What belongs here

**Yes:**

- Essay expansions on systems friction, civic access, or high-level public AI ethics
- Plain-language ELI5 intros and actionable checklists
- Links to public portfolio, ServeIT, EU policy primers, accessibility resources
- Typo fixes and broken-link reports

**No:**

- IRB, clinical, ProHealth, or 2050 research materials
- Private lab repo links (`jlzhao27` or similar)
- Bulk personal drafts from private working folders
- Travel reel content, itineraries, or non-public administrative details
- AI co-author trailers on commits

## How to submit changes

1. Open an issue describing the gap or proposed addition.
2. Fork and branch, or ask for write access on `zhao-langxi/madrid-ai-ethics`.
3. Keep tone plain and honest — outlines are fine; don’t invent metrics or experiences.
4. Run a privacy check: would this belong on a public LinkedIn post? If not, don’t commit it.

## Commit authorship

Commits should list the human contributor only ([Jade Zhao](https://github.com/jadexzhao)). No `Co-authored-by` trailers for AI tools.
