# Madrid · systems friction · public-service AI ethics

Spring 2026 working notebook from **Universidad Complutense de Madrid** (IU Overseas Study). A reader door for **systems friction** when you are the outsider in civic apps and public bureaucracy, plus reflective notes on **AI ethics in those services** (transparency, access before automation, human override).

**This is not an EU AI Act compliance project**, not legal advice, and not a controlled study. EU politics coursework supplied vocabulary for reading headlines; the notebook stays about observed friction and accountable design habits.

## Claim (locked)

Systems friction in public and civic digital services systematically disadvantages outsiders (non-native speakers, new arrivals, first-time users). Treating that friction as a first-class design requirement ... rather than a post-launch accessibility fix ... produces more accountable AI and digital public services.

Full lock + falsifiability: [`writeup/CLAIM.md`](writeup/CLAIM.md). Overview: [`RESEARCH.md`](RESEARCH.md).
